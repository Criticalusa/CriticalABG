### Key 2025 Changes Identified
- Atropine: 1.0 mg initial dose (from 0.5 mg)
- Vascular access: IV prioritized over IO in adults
- Compression depth: 2-2.4 inch range (max capped)
- Calcium: Discontinued routinely during arrest
- Blood glucose: 144-180 mg/dL post-ROSC target
- Coronary angiography: NOT routinely recommended post-ROSC
- Double sequential defibrillation: For refractory VF
- ECPR: Class 2a recommendation
- Unified Chain of Survival
