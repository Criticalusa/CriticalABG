## Context

**Situation:** Consultants navigating accountability-without-authority dynamic. Need to surface structural issues (gatekeeping, feedback loop gaps, consent timing) without triggering defensiveness.

**Audience layers:**
- Primary: Prassad (data-driven, sensitive to feedback, protector role) and Blanca (gatekept leadership position)
- Secondary: Internal leadership who need full visibility into the messaging strategy

**Tone requirements:**
- Calm, systems-focused, non-personal
- Plain language (explainable to a 14-year-old)
- Factual over emotional
- Protects relationships while surfacing structural concerns

**Source material:** Complete conversation prep document provided with:
- Opening/framing
- Problem statements
- Balanced feedback approach
- Specific asks
- Contingency responses for defensive reactions
- Delivery rules
- Internal strategic notes

## Constraints

- **Sensitivity**: Recipient (Prassad) is sensitive to feedback—structure and language must avoid triggering defensiveness
- **Format**: Must export cleanly to Word/Google Docs for leadership sharing
